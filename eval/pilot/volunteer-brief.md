# Volunteer brief

For the people who have agreed to take part in the pilot. Send it a few days ahead. Everything
marked **[Cytonn to confirm]** is a placeholder until Cytonn approves the wording.

## What this is

Cytonn is trying out a system that runs an aptitude test in a normal browser and gives a person
evidence if something about a sitting looks unusual. You are helping to see whether it works.
This is not a real recruitment test. Nothing you do here affects any application you have made.

You will sit a test of about 12 questions. Some volunteers have been asked to act out a few
cheating behaviours so the system has something to find. If that is you, you will have been told
privately. If you have not, just take the test the way you normally would.

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any point.

## What you need

- A Windows or Mac laptop or desktop with a **current Chrome or Edge** and a working webcam and
  microphone. Phones, tablets and Chromebooks cannot sit the test, and a private (incognito)
  window does not work. The system has only been tested on Windows.
- Your charger.
- Wi-Fi rather than mobile data. The recording uses about 40 to 50 MB an hour and never more
  than about 80 MB an hour.
- Your invitation link or code, which you will be given on the day.

## What is recorded

- **Your camera and microphone** for the whole test, at low resolution.
- **Your actions in the test window:** the answers you give, skips, when the window loses focus
  or you leave full screen, copy and cut attempts (only how many characters, never the text),
  and when you are idle.
- Nothing else. The system does not record your screen, your keystrokes, other windows or other
  programs.

Keep your microphone on for the whole test. If it is muted for a while you will see a warning
which will be shown to your reviewer.

## Before you start

- Turn off notifications: Focus assist or Do not disturb on Windows, a Focus on Mac. Close Teams,
  Slack, WhatsApp Web, your email and your calendar.
- The test asks for your camera and microphone once. Choose **Allow**.
- The test runs full screen. If you leave it, the question is covered until you press a button to
  come back. The clock does not stop.

## Who sees it, and for how long

A person (a reviewer) looks at anything unusual. No computer decides anything about you. The
system ranks the cases a reviewer should look at first, and a local program may write a short
note to help; it never gives a verdict.

A recording is deleted 30 days after a reviewer has made a decision about it. A recording that no
reviewer opens has no decision, so nothing deletes it automatically yet. **[Cytonn to confirm: what
happens to recordings nobody reviews, where recordings are stored, and who has access.]**

Your name is not stored with the recording or the test. You are known to the system by a
candidate number only. If you write a note about a technical problem, please do not put your
name or contact details in it.

## If something goes wrong

- Tab closed, or the browser crashed: open your link again. You come back to the same test and
  the clock has kept running.
- Camera or microphone blocked: the page tells you how to unblock it.
- Recording would not upload: stay on the upload page until it says it is finished. If you
  closed it, open your link again within 48 hours on the same computer and browser.
- Anything else: tell the person running the pilot, or use the complaint form at the end of the
  test. A person reads it. It does not count against you.
