## Taking the aptitude test: what you need to know before you register

### What you need

- A Windows or Mac laptop or desktop with a current **Chrome or Edge**, and a working webcam and
  microphone. Both must stay on for the whole test.
- Phones, tablets and Chromebooks cannot sit this test, and a private (incognito) window does not
  work. The test has been tested on Windows.
- A Wi-Fi connection. Your camera and microphone are recorded during the test, which uses about
  40 to 50 MB an hour and never more than about 80 MB an hour. Please do not use mobile data.

If you cannot meet these, contact **[Cytonn to confirm: name and address]** before the test so
that another way can be found.

### What happens on the day

You open your personal link. The test asks once for your camera and microphone, and you choose
Allow. It asks you to turn off notifications and to confirm you have, then to switch to full
screen. Only when your camera and microphone work does the test start. Questions come one at a
time, in order, and you cannot go back.

### What is recorded

- Your **camera and microphone**, for the whole test, at low resolution.
- Your **actions in the test window**: your answers, skips, when the window loses focus or you
  leave full screen, copy and cut attempts (only how many characters, never the text itself),
  and when you are idle.

Your screen, your keystrokes and your other programs are not recorded.

### Turn off notifications

A pop-up from a chat, an email or a calendar can pull the screen away from the test, and a
reviewer would see that as time away from the page. Before you start, switch on Focus assist (or
Do not disturb) on Windows, or a Focus on a Mac, and close Teams, Slack, WhatsApp Web, your
email and your calendar. A web page cannot see these settings, so we rely on you.

### Keep your microphone on

Keep your microphone on for the whole test. If it is muted for a while you will see a warning
which will be shown to your reviewer.

### Who sees it, and what is decided

A person, called a reviewer, looks at anything unusual. Leaving full screen or switching to
another window is noted and shown to them as information, not as a verdict. No computer decides
the outcome for you. The only automatic thing is that the test will not start until your camera
and microphone work.

### How long it is kept

Recordings are deleted 30 days after a reviewer makes a decision about them.

happens to recordings that are never reviewed.]**

During the test you are known to the system by a candidate number, not your name. Your name and
email address are kept only to send you your invitation and your result, and so the person running
the test can see who was invited. The people who review the test, and the computer programs that
help them, see only your candidate number. Your name and email are deleted with the test sitting.
You choose a password the first time you sign in. It is stored only in a scrambled form that nobody,
including the staff, can read back, and it is used only to sign you in to this test. If you forget it,
the person who invited you sends you a new link and you choose a new one.

### If something goes wrong

- If your camera or microphone stops, the test carries on and the clock keeps running. Check the
  cable and that no other program is using the camera.
- After the test your recording is sent to us. Keep the tab open until it says everything has
  been uploaded. If you closed it, open your link again within 48 hours on the same computer
  and browser and the rest will be sent.
- If your recording would not upload or your camera stopped working, use the form at the end of
  the test, or contact **[Cytonn to confirm]**. A person reads it, and it does not count against
  you.
